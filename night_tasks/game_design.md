## Objective: Balance and refine core mechanics
**Investigation:** Play through the core game loop. Identify points where the player feels stuck, bored, or where the difficulty spikes unfairly. Analyze the economic/resource flow.
**Action:** Tweak variables and rules to create a smoother progression curve. Ensure players have clear goals and feedback on their actions. Do not change the fundamental vision of the game—just polish the numbers and timings to make it more enjoyable.
